# Support

Tech crew doesn't collect fees from its members or for providing services to school groups. We typically focus fundraising efforts in tandem with larger drama productions, so look out for us in the lobby!

## Donations

We accept monetary donations [via OSP](https://osp.osmsinc.com/AnneArundel/BVModules/ProductTemplates/Bvc2013/Product.aspx?productid=A2013-VAR288).

## Electronics

Older, outdated, and soon to be e-wasted electronics can often be put to good use as our program expands its capabilities.

1. flat screen TVs and monitors -- any size, HDMI input required
2. laptops -- any size Windows, Mac, Chromebook, etc
3. tablets -- Windows, Android, Apple, etc
4. mini-computers like Intel NUC or Raspberry Pi
5. wireless routers -- singular or mesh
6. network switches -- bonus points for PoE

Computers and tablets need to power on, boot up, and have functional WiFi. Bonus points if they have an HDMI output. Power cables are much appreciated.

🔥 Portal devices must not have damaged/swollen lithium batteries.

⚠️ For any computer or tablet, please wipe or factory reset prior.

## Materials

Renovating? We might be interested in donation of used construction materials or fixtures. Before making a trip to the dumpster, [contact us](contact.html)!

Thanks for stopping by!
